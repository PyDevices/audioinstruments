# audioinstruments

audioinstruments is the part of OmniPython that plays: synthesizers, organs,
electric pianos and drum machines, each one a patch of modules on a virtual
rack, rendered a block at a time on audiodsp. It is the same on CPython and on
MicroPython-compatible firmware.

[Install it](get-started/install.md), then play
[your first note](get-started/first-program.md). The
[guides](guides/polyphony.md) take one task each, the
[reference](reference/instruments.md) describes every instrument, and
[How it works](how-it-works/index.md) explains the rack.
